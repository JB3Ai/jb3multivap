## TYRE & RIM ELITE INTRODUCTION

This policy document, the information you have provided and the proposal form, once accepted by us form the contract of insurance between us (MVIA a division of FSP 45790) and you (the insured). Upon receipt of the requisite premium, The Insurance Company will accept the risk if your vehicle complies with the terms and conditions as set out in your policy.

The Insurance Company reserves the right to decline this insurance, which will be done within 30 days of receipt of the proposal form. If we do decline, we will refund the full premium.

This plan consists of 2 Sections. The entire document including the “Terms and Conditions” and “Exclusions” is relevant to both sections Section 1 is the Insurance Section and Section 2 is the Non- Insurance Section which comprises of the Maintenance Portion and Additional Benefits.

UNFORESEEN ROAD HAZARDS (INSURANCE)

In the event the insured vehicle’s tyres sustain damage as a result of unforeseen road hazards on any maintained South African road this policy will pay for the repair or replacement of tyres up to the plan cover limit or cost of the tyre, whichever is the lesser.

BETTERMENT

Betterment for passenger vehicles will be determined using the table below.

Betterment for vehicles other than passenger vehicles will have betterment applied using the percentage of remaining tread.

RIM & MAG SCRATCHES (INSURANCE)

In the event the insured vehicle’s rims sustain scratches as a result of normal use on any maintained South African road we will pay for the repair of rim scratches as a result of damage sustained through the day to day use of the vehicle. Cracked or Buckled Rims are not covered.

Cover Limit

The individual cover limit for each benefit is stated in the schedule of benefits table.

Claim Limit

You are limited to a maximum of 2 tyres per claim and 1 claim per 90-day period. This is measured from the date of the most recent claim.

All repairs to damaged Tyres and/or Rims must be completed before a new claim will be accepted. Proof of the completed repairs must be provided in the event of a new claim.

Emergency Repairs

If emergency repairs or replacement is needed the benefit will be limited to 50% of the claim or 50% of the limit whichever is the lesser. Emergency Repairs are classified are repairs where it is not possible to register a claim prior to completing the repairs. All documentation, media and the damaged tyres must be retained as proof of the event.

WHEEL MAINTENANCE (NON-INSURANCE)

In order to extend the life of your tyres the following benefits are available after the applicable waiting period has expired. The individual waiting period for each maintenance benefit is shown in the schedule of benefits. Once a benefit is used, the waiting period before it may be used again is 12 months.

Wheel Balancing

In order to extend the life of your tyres we will pay for the balancing as per your schedule of benefits for your plan. You are eligible for this benefit when you make use of the tyre rotation benefit. The administrator will only pay market related prices.

Tyre Rotation

Rotation is necessary because of the uneven wear characteristics of each wheel position on the vehicle. Tyre rotation is vital to achieving even tread wear and long tread life and this benefit will assist in extending your tyre tread life. Please see the schedule for further information as to your vehicle’s eligibility. The administrator will only pay market related prices.

Nitrogen

This plan will cover the cost of filling all the wheels fitted to the insured vehicle with Nitrogen

## TYRE & RIM ADDITIONAL BENEFITS

+ ROADSIDE ASSISTANCE TOWING This benefit will pay for an authorised service provider to have your vehicle towed to the nearest approved facility. This benefit is subject to the occurrence of damage sustained as a result of an unforeseen road hazard where more than 1 wheel is damaged, or you are unable to continue further on your runflat tyres. Only administrator authorised towing services may be used.

24 Hour Roadside Assistance Number: (010) 211 5725

+ OVERNIGHT ACCOMMODATION

This benefit will reimburse you on receipt of your accommodation invoice and proof of payment. This benefit is subject to the occurrence of an insured event and more than 1 wheel is damaged or you are unable to continue further on your runflat tyres. The occurrence of the insured event must be more than 100km from your home.

+ FLAT TYRE ASSISTANCE This plan will cover the cost of the call out for the changing or repair of the tyre.

+ NITROGEN, WHEEL BALANCING & ROTATION This plan will cover the cost of nitrogen, balancing and rotation of all the wheels fitted to the insured vehicle. This benefit is subject to the occurrence of an insured event.

NUCLEAR RISKS

Loss, damage, or liability directly or indirectly caused by, or arising from, ionising radiation or radioactive contamination from any nuclear fuel or from any nuclear waste arising from burning nuclear fuel, or the radioactive, toxic, explosive or other dangerous properties of any explosive nuclear equipment or nuclear part of that equipment is not covered.

WAR AND PUBLIC DISORDER

Any loss, damage or liability caused by civil commotion, riot, strike, war, mutiny, military rising, martial law, rebellion, revolution, any act in protest against any State, Government or any Government body is not covered.

NOT COVERED UNDER THIS PLAN

1. Tyres where the remaining tyre tread is below the legal limit at the lowest point.

2. Resultant damage or consequential loss.

3. Loss or damage caused by road traffic accidents, fire, theft, driver abuse and or

negligence.

4. Any legal liability whatsoever to any third party.

5. Any repairs effected without a prior authorisation from the administrator.

6. Wheels of the incorrect size are excluded. This means that should you fit tyres or rims that

are not the same as the original tyres that the vehicle was made to use then these tyres will be excluded from cover.

7. Damage to the tyres if some other part of the vehicle (not rims or alloy wheels) is

damaged at the same time.

8. The Insured Items become unusable and irreparable as a result of vandalism, abuse,

mechanical irregularities of the vehicle and/or malicious damage.

9. Negligence of whatsoever nature resulting in damage to the covered item.

10. Damage to the rims where the remaining tyre tread is below the legal limit at the lowest

point.

11. Tyre or Rims fitted to the vehicle where an approved inspection was not completed or

were not fitted at inception of this policy.
