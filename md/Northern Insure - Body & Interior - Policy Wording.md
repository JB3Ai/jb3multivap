# BODY & INTERIOR

2 of 16

This document contains both the Northern Insure Body Maintenance and Northern Insure Interior Maintenance plans.

These products can be purchased separately or as a combination package. The product you have purchased will reflect on your transaction schedule.

Northern Insure Body Maintenance (MBM) and Northern Insure Interior Maintenance (MIM) are designed for the Proudly South African motorist who wishes to keep his vehicle in “showroom” condition and at the same time protect this valued investment against depreciation.

This document, the information you have provided and the proposal form, once accepted by us form a service contract between us (MVIA a division of FSP 45790) and you (the insured). Upon receipt of the requisite payment, The administrator will accept the contract if your vehicle complies with the terms and conditions as set out in your plan. This is not an insurance policy.

We reserve the right to decline this cover, which will be done within 30-days of receipt of the proposal form. If we do decline, we will refund the full payment, less inspection costs.

## BODY & INTERIOR INTRODUCTION

3 of 16

WHAT DOES THE BODY MAINTENANCE COVER?

The Northern Insure Body Maintenance covers damage to your vehicle caused from day to day motoring, such as scratches, dents & chips. This cover excludes any damage caused by vehicle accidents. The benefits are defined under the Body Maintenance section in this wording.

WHAT DOES THE INTERIOR MAINTENANCE COVER? The Northern Insure Interior Maintenance covers damage to your vehicle’s interior such as scratches, seat tears and unforeseen interior damage to specified components. The benefits are defined under the Interior Maintenance section in this wording.

IS THERE AN EXCESS WHEN CLAIM? No, there is no excess payable when you claim.

IS THERE A LIMIT TO DAMAGE THAT IS COVERED? Yes, the monetary limit of each claim is reflected on your Plan Schedule. Any damage exceeding your cover limit and quote allowance is not covered in this plan, refer to the Body Cover section for more details.

MUST I HAVE CAR INSURANCE? Yes, you must always have comprehensive insurance in place.

IS EXISTING DAMAGE COVERED? Yes, pre-existing damage is covered only on the Body Maintenance Plan, refer to the Body Cover section for more details.

WHERE CAN I TAKE MY VEHICLE TO BE REPAIRED? Any RMI approved repairer of your choice may be used.

CAN I HAVE MY VEHICLE REPAIRED AND THEN SEND YOU THE INVOICE? No, your claim must first be registered with the claims department and then approved before any repairs may begin on the vehicle.

## BODY & INTERIOR IMPORTANT DEFINITIONS

MAINTENANCE Repairs possible using the approved maintenance methods listed under the body or interior maintenance section of this plan.

ACCIDENTAL DAMAGE Means, collision damage sustained by a vehicle in motion.

INCIDENTAL DAMAGE Means, collision damage sustained by a stationary vehicle caused by an object in motion.

COST OF REPAIR Means the reasonable market related costs for components and labour to repair the damage sustained by the covered vehicle.

SCOPE OF REPAIR WORK REQUIRED Means the repair work required to return a vehicle to showroom condition.

SHOWROOM CONDITION Means a vehicle without any visible damage to the entire vehicle and which does not require any external repair or maintenance.

If the repairs required to return the vehicle to showroom condition (scope of repair) are greater than the cover limit and the quote allowance, it is not covered under this section. This means the scope of repair work must include all repairs necessary to return the vehicle to showroom condition

## BODY & INTERIOR BODY COVER

10 of 16

DAMAGE TO WHEEL RIMS AND MAG WHEELS Where your vehicle's wheel rims or mag wheels are scratched and able to be restored, we will pay for the repairs. Cracked or buckled rims are not covered.

You will be limited to 2 mags or rims per claim.

BUMPERS Cosmetic surface scratches to bumpers are covered under this benefit. Should the bumper be cracked, torn, ripped, broken or the mounting brackets affected then this benefit will not apply.

TAR REMOVAL We will pay for the removal of tar from the bodywork of your car. Normal industry detergents and polishes is the required method to be used.

AIR CONDITIONER TREATMENT To potentially remove the bacterial smells. This is available after the 1st 6-months and thereafter annually.

HEADLIGHT RENEWAL Plastic headlight renewal is limited to R500 per headlight and is only available after 6-months. This is limited to one benefit per plan.

SCRATCHES We will pay for repairs or restoration to surface scratches to the bodywork of the vehicle.

DENTS We will pay for the repair or restoration of minor dents which are repairable provided that the repair does not exceed the cover limit for the plan purchased. Where any part of the body panel has been ripped or torn the maintenance required shall not be defined as a dent and will not be repaired with this plan.

STONE CHIPS We will pay for the cost of maintenance or restoration of stone chips to the vehicle provided that the stone chips have not broken through to the metal.

WINDSCREEN AND GLASS COMPONENTS MAINTENANCE Windscreen chips which are repairable and do not affect the driver visibility and roadworthiness of the vehicle is covered.

If the windscreen cracks during the repair process, the replacement cost of the windscreen will be for your own account. This benefit includes repairable damage to headlamps and related glass components.

HAIL DAMAGE Where your vehicle is damaged by hail and can be successfully repaired by an accredited dent remover or recognised dent removal process for hail damage restoration. You will only be able to claim once under this benefit.

NUCLEAR RISKS

Loss, damage or liability directly or indirectly caused by, or arising from, ionising radiation or radioactive contamination from any nuclear fuel or from any nuclear waste arising from burning nuclear fuel, or the radioactive, toxic, explosive or other dangerous properties of any explosive nuclear equipment or nuclear part of that equipment.

WAR AND PUBLIC DISORDER

Any loss, damage or liability caused by civil commotion, riot, strike, war, mutiny, military rising, martial law, rebellion, revolution, any act in protest against any State, Government or any Government body.

EXCLUDED FROM COVER

The following are general exceptions that apply to the whole plan:

1. Any legal liability whatsoever to any third party.

2. Repairs that have not been authorised by us.

3. Damage caused by a road accident. This is not comprehensive insurance.

4. Damage to all electrical wiring, stickers, trimming, accessories, door mouldings, window

mouldings, beading, tyres, lamps of any sort or any window panel, car radios, sound equipment or car telephones.

5. Faults in workmanship or materials paid for, by us, on your behalf.

6. Abuse or negligence of any kind.

7. Any damage where the total repair costs exceed the cover limit and quote allowance.

8. Any damage falling outside the time limits of the period of this agreement.

9. Any damage resulting in the replacement of any vehicle part or accessory.

10. Any pre-existing interior damage.

11. Any pre-existing exterior damage which is not repairable using PDR methods.

12. Any rusting or any gradually developing cause.

13. Any depreciation in value arising from repairs or restoration or any other cause.

14. Any consequential loss from any cause whatsoever, or any contractual liability which you

may suffer or for which you may become liable.

15. Any maintenance required under this plan, which is in any way fraudulent or materially

inaccurate. In such circumstances, all payments made shall be forfeited.

EXCLUDED FROM COVER

1. Commercial Use: These are vehicle’s which perform a core function of the business. This

includes vehicles used for tradesman purposes such as plumbers, electricians etc.

2. Motoring Competition: Racing, track use, events or off-roading is not covered.

3. Carrying of explosives, driving instruction for reward, vehicle used for or in conjunction

with farming operations.

4. Emergency Services: This includes but is not limited to armed response, medical

assistance and traffic control vehicles.

5. Towing: Towing services are never covered.

6. Hire Vehicles: Vehicles which are owned by an entity for the purpose of generating rental

income.

7. Rebuilt and Code 3 vehicles: Vehicles which have been stolen, hijacked or involved in an

accident and registered as code 3.
