WHAT DOES A WARRANTY COVER?

A warranty provides cover for your vehicle’s components from sudden and unforeseen mechanical failure or electrical failure. Maintenance & servicing of the vehicle is not covered under a warranty.

WHICH COMPONENTS ARE COVERED?

The components covered are listed under the components covered section of this warranty.

WHICH COMPONENTS ARE NOT COVERED?

If an item is not listed under the components covered section, it is not covered.

IS THERE A WAITING PERIOD?

Yes, there is a waiting period of 90-days for the warranty portion of the plan. Damage occurring during or before this period is not covered on this plan. The waiting period can be waived if the vehicle is taken for an approved inspection.

MUST I TAKE MY VEHICLE FOR AN INSPECTION?

It is only a requirement to take your vehicle for an inspection if you have been specifically instructed to do so. You may elect to take your vehicle for an inspection in order to waive your waiting period.

MUST I SERVICE MY VEHICLE?

If your vehicle comes with a full-service history, your vehicle must continue to be serviced as per the manufacturer’s recommendations at any RMI approved workshop or dealership. If your vehicle does not have a full-service history, your vehicle's service history must be brought up to date. Please refer to “Your Responsibilities” for more information.

WHAT MUST I DO IN THE EVENT OF A BREAKDOWN?

Stop use of the vehicle immediately, contact the roadside assistance (dial: 010 211 5725) and have your vehicle towed to your nearest RMI approved repairer. Thereafter inform the repairer that you have a warranty with the administrator and contact our claims department to register your claim. Please refer to the claims section in this wording for more information.

MECHANICAL FAILURE

Means, the breaking or burning out of any of the components listed in the “Components Covered” section, as a result of a sudden and unforeseen breaking, failure or collapse of an insured part. For clarity, “breaking” or “broken” means components which are physically and forcibly separated into pieces or cracked.

ELECTRICAL FAILURE Electrical defects that cause a part to stop functioning is covered up to the component cover limit or the claim, whichever is the lesser.

FAILURE AS A RESULT OF WEAR & TEAR Mechanical failure of a covered component, during its expected lifespan, which is deemed to be as a direct result of wear and tear.

WEAR & TEAR The gradual degradation and unavoidable damage caused by aging or the normal use of a part.

FUNCTIONALLY DEFICIENT (WORN OUT)

A vehicle component which has not suffered mechanical or electrical failure, however, no longer functions in its intended manner. It has reached the end of its lifespan as result of normal use and must be replaced as part of routine vehicle maintenance.

COMPONENT A vehicle component can be a single part or a collection of parts. Each component covered is defined under the “Components Covered” section.

PRE-EXISTING DAMAGE Any damage which occurs during or before a waiting period has passed.

COST OF REPAIR Means the reasonable charges (market related) for components and labour to repair or replace the covered components listed.

RESULTANT DAMAGE This is failure of a part that is caused by faulty workmanship, an external force, or by the failure of any other component or part.

VEHICLE USE

The vehicle use that you chose is noted on your policy schedule. To have sufficient cover it’s vital that you insure your car for the correct use.

- Private or Personal Use: Private use is for private or social purposes, including driving

between your home and regular place of work only. The monthly limit for Private use is 3000km per month.

- Professional Use: Professional use includes private use and also includes the occasional

use of the vehicle to visit clients. Occasional, means not more than 2 times per week and the vehicle is not an essential part of the work function. The monthly limit for Professional use is 3500km per month.

- Business Use: Business use includes private use with additional cover, for instances

where the car forms an essential part of any work or function such as visiting clients on a daily basis excluding the transporting of goods and fare paying passengers. There is no monthly kilometre limit for Business use.

- Commercial Use: Commercial use is where the vehicle performs a core function of the

business. This includes the transporting of fare paying passengers, tourist vans and vehicles used for tradesman purposes such as plumbers, electricians etc. There is no monthly kilometre limit for Commercial use.

assistance and traffic control vehicles.
